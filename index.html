Photosynthesis


Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use light energy to convert carbon dioxide and water into chemical energy stored mainly as glucose.


Chlorophyll captures light energy, while oxygen is released as a by-product.


Carbon Dioxide + Water + Light Energy → Glucose + Oxygen
  • Sunlight provides energy.
  • Water is absorbed by the roots.
  • Carbon dioxide enters through leaves.
  • Chlorophyll captures light energy.
  • Glucose stores chemical energy.
  • Oxygen is released.
